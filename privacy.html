IP addresses, user-agent strings and precise locations are not stored. The site sets no cookies. The browser's session storage holds the random session string until the tab is closed. Local storage keeps your answers to the location and name questions, the name itself and the session strings of your last 20 visits, so that a name can be applied to, or removed from, all of them.

Purpose and legal basis

  • Anonymous visit records are used to display aggregated contact statistics on the page. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in understanding how the site is used, with data minimised so that individual visitors are not identified.
  • The approximate location is processed only after you choose “Share location” and confirm the browser prompt. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time with the “Location choice” link on the page; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.
  • The name is processed only if you enter it with “Sign your point”, and it is published next to your point. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can change or remove it at any time with “Sign your point” → “Remove name” in the same browser, or by writing to the address above. Please do not enter another person's name.

What is shown publicly

The page displays totals, counts per time zone and the time of the most recent visit. Visit points are drawn on a sphere; shared locations are rounded to whole degrees (about 100 km) before they leave the server. A name entered with “Sign your point” is shown publicly next to the point. Names that are offensive, impersonate someone or advertise may be removed without notice.

Storage and recipients

Records are stored in a Google Sheet through Google Apps Script, operated by Google as a service provider. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request data, including IP addresses, as described in its own privacy statement. Data is not sold or shared with other parties.

Retention

Records are deleted automatically 12 months after the visit.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Records do not contain IP addresses; unless you signed your point, it may only be possible to identify your record if you provide the approximate time of your visit. Requests can be sent to the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa.
